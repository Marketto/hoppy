Privacy policy

Version 1 — 4 November 2026

In short: Hoppy has no accounts, no advertising, no usage statistics or tracking, and sends no data to the author. Your data stays on your devices; on the local network only what is needed to find devices and to deliver the files you choose to send is shared. The only contacts with the internet are checking for and downloading updates from GitHub.

This policy covers the Hoppy apps (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad), the hoppy program and the download site, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

1. Who the author is and how to reach him

Hoppy is written by Marco Ricupero ("the author"). For questions about this policy open an issue at https://github.com/Marketto/hoppy/issues; issues are public, so do not include personal data.

The author does not collect, receive or keep personal data of Hoppy users: he has no servers taking part in transfers. The data described in section 2 is processed by your device, under your control; for the data of sections 4 and 5 the controller is GitHub.

2. Data kept on your device

Hoppy stores only on the device, without copying it anywhere:

  • the device name (the computer's or phone's, can be changed) and a random identifier (UUID) created on first start;
  • a cryptographic key of the device, created on first start, which never leaves the device and is never included in backups;
  • the list of trusted devices: for each one its identifier, name, key fingerprint, date of first contact, whether you verified it and the last network address used;
  • the settings: theme, music and sounds, automatic receiving, time of the last update check and the like;
  • on computers, a diagnostic log (hoppy.log, at most about 512 KB) with technical events, numbers and sizes of files, device names and IP addresses; it never contains file names, paths or contents. The Android, iPhone and iPad apps do not write a log of their own;
  • the files you receive and accept.

How to delete it. On Android, iPhone and iPad uninstalling the app deletes the settings, the key and the list of trusted devices (on iPhone and iPad also the received files, unless you moved them); on Android received files stay in Download/Hoppy until you delete them. On computers uninstall Hoppy and then delete the folders in the table; on Linux and macOS also the autostart and right-click entries that hoppy uninstall removes. You can also forget a single device from the Trusted devices menu.

3. Data shared on the local network

To be found, Hoppy periodically announces on the local network, to every device connected to the same network, the device name, its random identifier, the operating system and the network port; anyone on the network also sees your IP address. These announcements are not encrypted. The Android, iPhone and iPad apps do this only while they are open or ready to receive.

When you send files:

  • the files go only to the device you pick and only after the recipient accepts;
  • before the transfer the receiving device sees your device name, the names and sizes of the files, and your device's public key (its "fingerprint"); if the recipient accepts or verifies the code, their device remembers it in its list of trusted devices (and yours does the same with theirs);
  • with current versions (protocol v1) file names, sizes and contents are end-to-end encrypted between the two devices; with old apps (v0) they travel in clear and the app marks this with ⚠: anyone on the same network could read them.

None of this goes through the internet or the author.

4. Checking for and downloading updates (GitHub)

The apps check whether a new version exists at most every few hours (on computers also at start, after waking up and when the menu opens, with rate limits) by asking api.github.com for the latest published version. When you choose to update, the file is downloaded from github.com, which serves it from domains such as objects.githubusercontent.com. The requests contain only the technical data of an ordinary web connection: your IP address, the time and the Hoppy version (in the User-Agent header). No device or user identifier is sent. Development builds do not check.

GitHub processes this data as an independent controller, under its own privacy statement. If you do not want these contacts you can block them with a firewall; Hoppy keeps working on the local network.

5. Download site

The download site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs technical data of visits (for example the IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement. The page asks api.github.com for the latest available version; the fonts are hosted on the site itself. The site uses no cookies and no analytics; in the browser's storage (localStorage) it keeps only the language you picked and whether you turned the music on, which you can clear from the browser's settings.

6. Third-party tools

To install Hoppy on iPhone and iPad you can use third-party tools such as Sideloadly or SideStore (which reads the list of versions from the download site and downloads the app from GitHub); installing and updating on Android goes through the operating system. These tools, the stores and the operating systems have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

7. Legal bases and retention

  • The data of section 2 is processed on your device at your request, to make Hoppy work, and stays until you delete it; the diagnostic log overwrites itself (one file plus one backup copy).
  • The announcements and transfers of section 3 happen at your request, when you open Hoppy or send files; the receiving device keeps your name and fingerprint until its user forgets you.
  • Update checks are based on the legitimate interest in receiving fixes, including security fixes; the data is kept by GitHub under its privacy statement.

8. Your rights

You have the rights of articles 15–22 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection). Since the author holds no data about you, you exercise them directly on the device (changing the name, forgetting devices, deleting the folders of section 2) and, for the data of sections 4 and 5, with GitHub. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (https://www.garanteprivacy.it).

9. Children

Hoppy collects no data about anyone, children included. Children under 14 should use it with the help of an adult.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version gets a new number and a new date and the app shows it to you again. Earlier versions remain in the history of the project's repository.
